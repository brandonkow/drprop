This is a historical annual edition of the recurring Market Pulse series, not monthly data or a claim to show the latest publication. Aggregate transaction value combines transaction counts and values. It is not an asking-price series, median house price, price index, forecast or investment return. The video displays the metric, geography, units, years, source and retrieval date and labels these as observed transactions. The separate sample JSON remains explicitly illustrative.
